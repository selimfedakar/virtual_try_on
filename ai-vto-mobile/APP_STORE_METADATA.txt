VTO lets you try on any outfit before you buy it — powered by AI.

Just snap a selfie and pick a garment you love — from your camera roll or by
pasting a product link. VTO's AI shows you exactly how that piece would look on
you in seconds.

WHY VTO?
• No more returns — see the fit before you commit
• Tops, bottoms and full-body outfits — pick the right category for the best result
• Ask the AI Stylist: it analyzes any garment and suggests 3 ways to wear it
• Build your Closet: categorize garments, combine them into outfits
• Share watermarked looks and post them to the VTO community
• Fit Analysis: size estimates from your own measurements

HOW IT WORKS
1. Take a selfie in-app (front camera)
2. Add a garment from your camera roll — or paste a product URL
3. Choose the garment type and tap Preview — AI does the rest in seconds
4. Save, share, post to the community, or ask the AI Stylist for advice

FEATURES
→ Photorealistic AI try-on powered by a state-of-the-art try-on model
→ AI Stylist: real garment analysis with personalized outfit suggestions
→ Closet 2.0: categorized wardrobe, outfit builder, daily outfit reminder
→ Community: share your best looks, like and browse other stylists
→ Fit Analysis: measurement-based size recommendations
→ High-quality generation mode for Premium members

VTO PREMIUM
Free members get 5 AI generations per day. Premium unlocks unlimited
generations and high-quality mode. Subscriptions are billed through your
App Store account and can be managed or cancelled anytime in Settings.

Your privacy matters: photos you select are securely sent to our AI service
only to generate your preview, and are never shared with third parties for
marketing.

Download VTO and never buy the wrong outfit again.

Steps to test:
1. Open the app (first launch shows a short onboarding) and sign in with the
   credentials above.
2. On the Try On tab, tap Camera to take a selfie (front camera).
3. Pick a garment photo from the library — or tap "Try a sample garment" /
   "Paste product link".
4. Select the garment type (Top / Bottom / Full Body) and tap "Preview My
   Outfit" — the AI produces the try-on result in ~10-60 seconds.
5. Visit the Style tab for AI garment analysis and outfit suggestions.
6. Visit the Closet tab for past results, wardrobe categories and outfits;
   the Share tab for watermarked sharing and the community feed.
7. Content can be reported from the result screen ("Flag Inappropriate
   Content") — reports are stored and reviewed.

Notes:
- The AI generation step calls an external try-on API (Fashn.ai) and may take
  up to 60 seconds under load. This is expected behavior.
- The AI Stylist calls a vision LLM server-side; free accounts get 10 stylist
  requests/day.
- Subscriptions: monthly (and annual when configured) via RevenueCat; free
  tier is 5 generations/day.
